This document establishes the authoritative, uncompromised technical roadmap for Jacobik. It serves two distinct purposes:

## 2. Architecture Review: Current Invariants & Production Pipeline

Jacobik is an inverse-kinematics (IK) conformer generator for transition-metal and lanthanide complexes. It takes metal identity, total charge, spin multiplicity, coordination geometry label, and one SMILES per ligand with donor indices, and produces a ranked conformer ensemble without force fields or molecular dynamics.

### 2.1 The Unified $u1$ Objective Function
The $u1$ profile employs **one identical function** for gradient optimization and conformer ranking:

$$\mathcal{S}(\mathbf{X}) = \frac{1}{Z} \left[ Q_1 + Q_2 + S_{\text{orient}} + Q_5 + Q_6 + l_{\text{bend}} + NCI + QV \right]$$

where:
- Every active term is expressed in **nats** (dimensionless log-likelihood).
- Every term enters with **weight 1.0**.
- $Z = N_{\text{Q1}} + N_{\text{Q2}} + N_{\text{SO}} + N_{\text{Q5}}$ is the exact count of internal geometric features, normalizing the Adam step size across complexes without altering the argmax.
- Shape deviations $C_{\text{shape}}$, symmetry ties $C_{\text{sym}}$, and macrocycle seams are augmented-Lagrangian constraints, not score terms.

### 2.2 Active Quantities: Mathematical Models, Derivation, and Physical Floors

#### 1. Q1: Metal–Donor Distance
* **Variable:** $d_s = \|\mathbf{D}_s - \mathbf{M}\|$, distance from metal to donor centroid.
* **Data Derivation:** Cleaned tmQM/LnQM crystals. Keyed by ladder `m_sub_early`:
  $$(\text{metal}, \text{spin}, \text{OS}, \text{subclass}, \text{geom}, \text{trans}) \to \dots \to (\text{element\_any})$$
  Trans partner assigned when polyhedral site angle $>140^\circ$ ($\cos \theta < -0.766$).
* **Model:** 1D Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM), $K \le 4$ (BIC), with Reynolds MAP partial pooling ($\alpha_k = \frac{n_k}{n_k + r}$).
* **Thermal Floor:** Clamped to donor-element 298 K GFN2-Hessian thermal widths:
  $$\sigma_{T, \text{Q1}} \in \{\text{C: } 0.0406, \text{P: } 0.0535, \text{eta: } 0.0571, \text{Cl: } 0.0577, \text{O: } 0.0597, \text{I: } 0.0598, \text{Br: } 0.0650, \text{S: } 0.0693, \text{N: } 0.0705\}\text{ \AA}$$
* **Boundary Continuation:** Beyond $d_{\text{hi}} = \max_j (\mu_j + 3\sigma_j)$, adds a smooth one-sided continuation wall:
  $$\Delta \log p = -\frac{1}{2} \left(\frac{d - d_{\text{hi}}}{\max(\sigma_j, \sigma_T)}\right)^2$$

#### 2. Q2: Donor–Metal–Donor Bite and Polyhedral Angles
* **Variable:** $c_{st} = \cos \angle(\mathbf{D}_s, \mathbf{M}, \mathbf{D}_t)$ for all site pairs.
* **Data Derivation:** Keyed by `(geometry, distance_class, metal, subclass_A, subclass_B, site_pair_class)`. Distance classes: `chelate_short` ($\le 4$ bonds), `chelate_long` ($\le 8$), `different_ligand`.
* **Model:** Mixture of Student-$t$ distributions in cosine space ($\nu \in \{2, 4, 10\}$):
  $$p(c) = \sum_k w_k \frac{\Gamma((\nu+1)/2)}{\sqrt{\pi \nu \sigma_k^2}\Gamma(\nu/2)} \left(1 + \frac{(c - \mu_k)^2}{\nu \sigma_k^2}\right)^{-(\nu+1)/2}$$
* **Thermal Floor:** Floored to $\sigma_{T, \text{Q2}} = 4.9^\circ$ ($0.0861\text{ rad}$) projected into cosine space:
  $$\operatorname{Var}(c) \ge \sin^2(\mu_k) \sigma_T^2 + \frac{1}{2}\cos^2(\mu_k)\sigma_T^4$$

#### 3. $S_{\text{orient}}$ (QO): Joint Donor Approach and Haptic Orientation
* **Variables:**
  - **Kappa Donors:** Gram matrix of unit bond vectors $\mathbf{g} = (\mathbf{u}_M \cdot \mathbf{u}_i, \mathbf{u}_i \cdot \mathbf{u}_j)$ in dimension $d \in \{1, 3, 6\}$.
  - **Haptic Donors ($\eta^2\text{--}\eta^8$):** Face coordinates $\mathbf{g} = (\text{tilt}, \text{bend}, \text{slip})$.
    * $\text{tilt} = \mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{u}_M$: face normal against centroid-to-metal vector.
    * $\text{bend} = \frac{1}{|S|} \sum_{i \in S} \frac{\mathbf{X}_i - \mathbf{A}_i}{\|\mathbf{X}_i - \mathbf{A}_i\|} \cdot \mathbf{n}$: substituent lean.
    * $\text{slip} = \frac{\operatorname{std}_i(\|\mathbf{M} - \mathbf{F}_i\|)}{\operatorname{mean}_i(\|\mathbf{M} - \mathbf{F}_i\|)}$: lateral off-centering of the metal relative to the face ring atoms.
* **Model:** Full-covariance GMM ($K \le 4$, BIC). Subsets: $\eta^2, \eta^3$ fit `[bend, slip]`; $\eta^4\text{--}\eta^8$ fit all three `[tilt, bend, slip]`.
* **Thermal Floor:** Loewner floor $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_k \ge \operatorname{diag}(\sigma_{T, \text{SO}}^2)$, $\sigma_{T, \text{SO}} = 4.2^\circ$ for kappa donors. (Haptic thermal width calibration is detailed in Section 4.5).

#### 4. Q5: Ligand Backbone Torsions
* **Variable:** Signed IUPAC dihedrals $\phi \in [-\pi, \pi)$ for rotatable single bonds.
* **Data Derivation:** 4-atom environment + chelate context. Folded by rotor symmetry (3-fold for $-\text{CH}_3$, 2-fold for planar aryls).
* **Model:** von Mises Mixture (VMM) with well-merging:
  $$p(\phi) = \sum_k w_k \frac{\exp(\kappa_k \cos(\phi - \mu_k))}{2\pi I_0(\kappa_k)}$$
* **Thermal Cap:** $\kappa_k \le \frac{1}{\sigma_{T, \text{Q5}}^2} = 17.3$ ($\sigma_{T, \text{Q5}} = 13.8^\circ = 0.2402\text{ rad}$).

#### 5. Q6: Repulsive Sterics & Analytical Cores
* **Variable:** Non-bonded atom pairs $>3$ bonds apart.
* **Model:**
  - **Class-5 Non-Polar Pairs:** Shifted-force Buckingham exponential to Alvarez vdW radii $r_m = R_i + R_j$:
    $$U(r) = \frac{A_{ij}}{RT} \left[ e^{-b_{ij} r} - e^{-b_{ij} r_m} + b_{ij} e^{-b_{ij} r_m}(r - r_m) \right] \quad (r < r_m), \quad U(r) = 0 \quad (r \ge r_m)$$
    Combining rules: $A_{ij} = \sqrt{A_i A_j}$, $b_{ij} = \frac{b_i + b_j}{2}$. Continued linearly for $U > 50\text{ kT}$.
  - **Class-4, Metal, and Polar-H Pairs:** Analytical onset core $w_{\text{core}}(r) = \kappa (e^{b(r_0 - r)} - 1)$ below 0.1% data quantile $r_0$. For polar $H_p\cdots A$, $r_0 \approx 1.66\text{ \AA}$ preserves the hydrogen-bond approach channel.

#### 6. Bend Prior: Valence Angle Bends
* **Variable:** Valence bond angle deformations $\beta_k$ along chelate backbones.
* **Model:** Harmonic restraint with a $2^\circ$ flat-bottom deadband:
  $$l_{\text{bend}} = -\frac{1}{2 RT} \sum_k k_k \max(0, |\beta_k| - 2^\circ)^2$$
  Stiffness parameters $k_k \in \{310, 1100, 1300, 1800\}\text{ kJ/mol/rad}^2$ from MACE single points. Divided by $Z$.

#### 7. QV / BVS: Bond Valence Coupling
* **Variable:** Bond valence sum $V = \sum_{s \in \sigma} \exp\left(\frac{R_{0, s} - d_s}{0.37}\right)$.
* **Model:** Harmonic reference ratio penalty for early transition metals and lanthanides:
  $$QV = -\frac{1}{2} c \left(\frac{V}{\text{OS}} - \mu\right)^2$$

### 2.3 The Principle of Score Balance: Curvature Alignment
Linear re-weighting of score terms ($w_i \cdot Q_i$) was previously tested and failed catastrophically: top-1 accuracy collapsed from 0.47 to 0.32 because scaling down nats flattens the wide tails that veto unphysical geometries.

In `u1`, balance is established strictly in **second-derivative (Hessian) space**:
$$\kappa_{\text{ratio}} = \frac{\partial^2 E_{\text{MACE}} / \partial q^2}{\partial^2 \mathcal{S} / \partial q^2} \approx 1.0 \quad \text{along all soft degrees of freedom } q$$
Clamping mixture variances to measured 298 K thermal widths ($\sigma_T$) eliminates artificial statistical "needles" (components with $\sigma \sim 0.005\text{ \AA}$) and brings all term curvatures into the physical window ($0.7\text{--}1.5$):
$$\text{Q1: } 1.39, \quad \text{Q2: } 0.99, \quad S_{\text{orient}}: 1.01, \quad \text{Q5: } 1.11, \quad \text{Q6: } 1.43$$
with a stiff-direction Spearman rank correlation of **$0.867$** against MACE64 energy curvature.

### 2.4 Traditional Conformer Generator Architecture: Isomer Classes vs. Thermal Populations
Jacobik does not output an abstract, single-scalar Boltzmann average. It operates as a **classical hierarchical conformer ensemble generator**:

1. **Discrete Isomer Classes (Top-Level Topology):**
   - Distinct coordination polyhedral assignments (cis/trans, fac/mer, $\Delta/\Lambda$).
   - Binding modes (hapticity $\eta^1$ vs $\eta^3$, bridging modes).
   - Ligand internal configuration (stereocenters, backbone E/Z isomers, macrocycle fold topologies).
2. **Intra-Isomer Thermal Populations (Continuous Manifolds):**
   - Within each discrete isomer class, continuous conformational space is sampled along flexible dihedrals, chelate ring puckers, and haptic face motions via beam search and joint Adam IK optimization.
   - Outputs unweighted, deduplicated conformer snapshots representing accessible local minima.

#### Structural Quality Metrics
The dual nature of the search space dictates our ranking metrics:
* **Inter-Isomer Ranking (`rho_isomer`):** Spearman correlation between class-best score and class-best MACE energy across distinct coordination isomers ($0.764$ on dev80).
* **Intra-Isomer Ranking (`rho_within`):** Spearman correlation between score and energy *within* the same isomer class ($0.685$ on dev80).
* **Global Ranking (`rho_all`, `rho_lo`):** Correlation across the entire conformer pool ($0.704$), and restricted to $<50\text{ kJ/mol}$ of the floor ($0.505$).

## 3. Work Domain 1: General Data-Derived NCI Architecture

### 3.1 Scientific Rationale: Resolving the Inverted Boltzmann Saturation Paradox
In structural databases, observation frequencies saturate at $P \approx 1.0$ once an interaction is favorable by $\ge 4\text{--}5\ k_B T$. A strong ionic hydrogen bond ($-60\text{ kJ/mol}$) cannot be observed $10^{10}$ times more frequently than a modest hydrogen bond ($-12\text{ kJ/mol}$).

Therefore, the raw empirical log-odds $L(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}) = \log \frac{p_{\text{ref}}(\mathbf{x} \mid \mathbf{z})}{p_{\text{base}}(\mathbf{x} \mid \mathbf{z})}$ provides the **exact geometric well shape and location**, but suffers from **dynamic range compression**. A general calibration framework must map these dimensionless nats into physical energy without relying on pre-coded heuristics.

```
       [Raw Graph Motif z] ──► [Matched Reference vs Baseline] ──► [L(x, z) = log(p_ref / p_base)]
                                                                               │
                                                                               ▼
       [Automated Energy Calibration Engine] ◄────────────────────── [C² Spline u(x, z)]
       ├─ 1. GFN2 Hessian Compliance Projection:  c_curv(z) = kT / (σ_T,z² · K_stat)
       ├─ 2. Subsystem Motion Energy Response:    c_depth(z) from ΔE_MACE regression
       └─ 3. Hierarchical Bayesian MAP Pooling:   c(z) shrinks to parent ladder on sparse data
                               │
                               ▼
       Total Calibrated Term: E_NCI(x, z) = c(z) · u(x, z) / Z
```

---

### 3.2 Matched Reference-versus-Baseline Formulation
For any graph-defined non-bonded anchor pair $(A, B)$ separated by $>3$ bonds or across ligands:
1. **Target Corpus:** Cleaned tmQM X1–X3 and LnQM LNf records (with previously unscanned charged shards audited or explicitly accounted for).
2. **Control Baseline Poses:** Poses generated by the frozen control solver `u1_pure_rep` (pure repulsion, NCI off, seeds 11, 12, 13) on the identical parent-state.
3. **Log-Enrichment Surface:**
   $$L(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}) = \log \frac{p(\mathbf{x} \mid \text{reference}, \mathbf{z})}{p(\mathbf{x} \mid \text{baseline}, \mathbf{z})}$$
   where $\mathbf{x}$ is the continuous interaction geometry and $\mathbf{z}$ is the local graph context.
4. **Support Mask & Lower Confidence Bound:**
   $$\operatorname{supp}(\mathbf{z}) = \{ \mathbf{x} : N_{\text{ref}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}) \ge 10 \ \land \ N_{\text{base}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}) \ge 10 \}$$
   $$u(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}) = \max\left(0, \ \hat{L}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}) - 1.96 \cdot \operatorname{SE}(\hat{L}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}))\right)$$
   Outside $\operatorname{supp}(\mathbf{z})$, $u \equiv 0$. Negative enrichment earns zero attraction (Q6 owns repulsion).
5. **$C^2$ Spline with Boundary Taper:** Interpolated via penalized Poisson $C^2$ Hermite splines, with a $C^2$ quintic taper enforcing $u = 0$, $\nabla u = \mathbf{0}$, and $\nabla^2 u = \mathbf{0}$ at the boundary.

---

### 3.3 Multiplicity & Sublinear Aggregation
To prevent unphysical binding energy accumulation from multiple competing partners:
1. **Competing Acceptors for One Donor–H:**
   $$A_h = \tau \log \left( 1 + \sum_{a} \left[ \exp\left(\frac{u_{ha}}{\tau}\right) - 1 \right] \right), \quad \tau = 1.0$$
   Saturates sublinearly, capturing bifurcated H-bonds without double counting.
2. **Sibling Methyl C–H Sibling Softmax:**
   Protons on the same methyl carbon are gated by $\max(0, -\cos \theta_{CH})^3$ and aggregated with $\tau = 0.15$.

---

### 3.4 Automated, Data-Derived Energy Calibration Engine
For any motif $\mathbf{z}$ (predefined or discovered from data), its physical energy scale $c(\mathbf{z})$ is derived automatically through two coupled physical observables and hierarchical shrinkage:

1. **Compliance Curvature Projection (Hessian Matching):**
   Using the precomputed GFN2 compliance matrices $\mathbf{C} = k_B T \mathbf{H}^+$ from `thermal_widths.json`:
   $$\sigma_{T, \mathbf{z}}^2 = \mathbf{J}_{\mathbf{z}} \mathbf{C} \mathbf{J}_{\mathbf{z}}^T, \quad K_{\text{phys}}(\mathbf{z}) = \frac{k_B T}{\sigma_{T, \mathbf{z}}^2}, \quad c_{\text{curv}}(\mathbf{z}) = \frac{K_{\text{phys}}(\mathbf{z})}{\left. \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial \mathbf{x}^2} \right|_{\mathbf{x}_0}}$$
   *Tight, strong interactions automatically yield large $c(\mathbf{z})$; floppy dispersion interactions automatically yield small $c(\mathbf{z})$.*
2. **Subsystem Motion Energy Regression (Well-Depth Matching):**
   Across 5-point displacement scans on calibration complexes, evaluate:
   $$\Delta E_{\text{target}}(\mathbf{x}_k, \mathbf{z}) = \Delta E_{\text{MACE}}(\mathbf{x}_k) - \Delta E_{\text{base}}(\mathbf{x}_k)$$
   $$\hat{c}_{\text{depth}}(\mathbf{z}) = \frac{\sum_k \Delta E_{\text{target}}^{(k)} \cdot \Delta u^{(k)}}{\sum_k (\Delta u^{(k)})^2}$$
3. **Hierarchical Bayesian MAP Pooling:**
   To safely handle rare or newly discovered motifs, $c(\mathbf{z})$ shrinks toward its parent category in the graph ladder:
   $$c(\mathbf{z}) = \frac{N_{\mathbf{z}}}{N_{\mathbf{z}} + \tau_{\text{pool}}} \hat{c}(\mathbf{z}) + \frac{\tau_{\text{pool}}}{N_{\mathbf{z}} + \tau_{\text{pool}}} c_{\text{parent}}$$

### 3.5 Computational Feasibility & Runtimes
* **Baseline Pose Library (One-Time Cache):** 240 parents $\times$ 3 seeds generated by `u1_pure_rep` on GPU takes $\mathbf{\sim 15\text{--}20\text{ minutes}}$ total. Cached permanently as `.npz` files.
* **Extraction:** $<45\text{ seconds}$ on the 240 cohort.
* **Spline Fitting ($C^2$ Poisson):** Sparse banded linear solves take $\sim 15\text{ ms}$ per table; 100 tables take $\mathbf{\sim 1.5\text{ seconds}}$.
* **Compliance Projection:** Matrix-vector products using precomputed $\mathbf{H}^+$ take $\mathbf{< 1\text{ second}}$.
* **MACE Calibration Motions:** 200 single points on GPU take $\mathbf{\sim 15\text{ seconds}}$.
* **Total Addition to Routine `run_refit.py fit` Stage:** **$\mathbf{< 2\text{ minutes}}$**, with peak RAM $<650\text{ MiB}$.

### 3.6 Pitfalls, Uncertainties, and Necessary Evidence
1. **Multi-Body Environment Residuals (Literature / Empirical Citation):**
   *Evidence:* In the 2026-10-19 audit (`nci_hb_physical_latest_diagnosis_2026-10-19.md`), DOVYES showed an isolated-pair analytic error of $-87.1\text{ kJ/mol}$, but an opposing environment residual of $+90.0\text{ kJ/mol}$.
   *Mitigation:* Calibration motions must be measured in the context of the coordinated complex, not isolated gas-phase dimers.
2. **Co-Bound Metal Donor/Acceptor Contamination:**
   *Evidence:* The 2026-10-18 census revealed artificial peaks at $2.5\text{ \AA} / 90^\circ$ caused by cis-coordinated ligands.
   *Mitigation:* Co-bound donor/acceptor pairs (both directly bonded to the metal) are strictly excluded from NCI candidate generation (owned by Q2).
3. **Data Sparsity in Multi-Dimensional Splines:**
   *Uncertainty:* 3D splines $(r, \theta, \phi)$ in thin angular slices can suffer from empty bins.
   *Mitigation:* Enforce empirical Poisson smoothing with zero boundary conditions and minimum observation counts ($N_{\min} \ge 10$).

### 4.1 S3 Search Coverage & Context-Aware Beam Search
* **Defect:** `ik/beam.py` expands rotatable bonds across Q5 modes scored by isolated ligand dihedrals (`BEAM_MODE_SCORE="density"`), completely blind to the metal, coordination polyhedron, and sibling ligands. In 16/80 complexes, 34 joints suffer from the "needle-mode bug," where ultra-sharp secondary wells prevent seeding the dominant broad well.
* **Solution:**
  1. Evaluate partial beam paths with composite score $Q_5 + Q_{6, \text{rep}}(\text{placed atoms}, \mathbf{M}, \text{ligands})$, pruning clashing branches early.
  2. Decouple torsion rows so distinct coordination isomer classes explore distinct torsional trajectories.
  3. Eliminate complexes with seed spread $>50\text{ kJ/mol}$ (KIQYEQ, OVODUZ, FIGHOT).

### 4.2 S4 Ligand Internals: The 61% Floor Lever
As proven in METHODOLOGY, frozen ligand internals account for **34% to 61% of the floor error**.
* **Solution A:** Generalized bound-state (H-capped) embedding for all coordinating anionic groups (extending the group-13 `bound_surrogate` to amides, thiolates, and enolates).
* **Solution B:** Small-ring pucker enumeration (chair, boat, envelope) and macrocycle fold pools during free-ligand embedding.
* **Solution C:** Systematic enumeration of unassigned stereocenters and open double-bond E/Z isomers.

### 4.3 S2 Bond Valence Revision & Tail Protection
Revise the bond-valence coupling term ($QV$): key $R_0$ and $V$ levels by coordination number and spin, exclude $\pi$-acceptor carbon donors, and restore tail protection for fluxional lanthanides and early transition metals.

### 4.4 Haptic $S_{\text{orient}}$ Thermal Width Calibration
* **Open Gap:** In `scorer.py`, haptic $S_{\text{orient}}$ features (`tilt`, `bend`, `slip`) currently lack physical thermal compliance floors and rely on fit-time empirical covariance regularizers (`COV_FLOOR = 0.05**2`).
* **Work Item:** Measure 298 K GFN2-Hessian thermal widths for haptic face degrees of freedom across the 300 Hessian training complexes and install Loewner-order thermal floors.
