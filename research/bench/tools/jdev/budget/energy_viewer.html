How to read the map

Force arrows point in the direction that lowers total MACE energy locally. Red energy colors mean a higher model-local atomic contribution than the selected baseline; blue means lower. Bond colors are red for lengthening and blue for shortening, which does not by itself establish whether a bond is strained.

The modes here are named ligand translations, rotations, torsions and contact openings, not vibrational eigenmodes. All measurements use the same float64 MACE model. Reference structures need not have zero MACE force.
